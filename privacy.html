YT Stream Scheduler

Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-10

YT Stream Scheduler is a personal tool. Only its developer uses it, to schedule live streams on the developer's own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, it asks for the YouTube scope youtube.force-ssl. It uses that access only to:

  • create, list, bind and delete upcoming live broadcasts on your channel;
  • list your live streams (id, title and resolution, never the stream key);
  • set the category and tags of a broadcast and upload its thumbnail.

What it stores

  • The sign-in token is stored on the developer's computer and as an encrypted secret in the developer's private GitHub repository. During a GitHub Actions run, it is also written to that run's temporary machine, which is discarded when the run ends.
  • It stores no other data from your Google or YouTube account. It has no server, no analytics and no ads.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone. The tool sends data only to YouTube, through the YouTube API.

YT Stream Scheduler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube and Google

YT Stream Scheduler uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.
